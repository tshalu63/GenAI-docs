# 05. Large Language Models (LLMs)

Large Language Models (LLMs) are **Generative AI systems designed to understand and produce human-like text**.

---

## 1. What Are LLMs?
- Models trained on **massive text corpora**.  
- Examples: GPT-4, LLaMA, PaLM.  
- Capable of:
  - Text completion  
  - Question answering  
  - Summarization  
  - Code generation

---

## 2. Key Components
- **Transformer Architecture:** Core for sequence modeling.  
- **Tokenization:** Breaking text into smaller units.  
- **Embeddings:** Represent words or tokens in vector space.  
- **Fine-Tuning:** Adapting pre-trained models to specific tasks.

---

## 3. Role in Generative AI
- LLMs serve as the **foundation for creative text applications**, chatbots, coding assistants, and multimodal AI systems.

---

## 4. Summary
LLMs demonstrate **the power of scale in Generative AI**, capturing knowledge and producing creative, human-like text outputs.
