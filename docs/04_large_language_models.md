# 04. Transformers and Attention Mechanism

Transformers are **the foundation of modern Generative AI**, enabling scalable and context-aware generation.

---

## 1. Attention Mechanism
- Focuses on **important parts of the input sequence** while generating outputs.  
- Example: In translating a sentence, the model pays attention to words with relevant meaning.  
- **Benefit:** Captures long-range dependencies in text, images, or audio.

---

## 2. Transformer Architecture
- **Components:**
  - **Encoder:** Understands the input.  
  - **Decoder:** Generates the output.  
  - **Self-Attention:** Determines relationships between elements in the input.  
- **Advantages:** Handles long sequences, parallel processing, and scalable training.

---

## 3. Role in Generative AI
- Transformers power:
  - **Text generation:** GPT, BERT-based models.  
  - **Image generation:** Text-to-image models (e.g., DALL·E uses transformers for cross-modal attention).  
  - **Multimodal tasks:** Audio, video, and text combined.

---

## 4. Summary
Transformers and attention mechanisms **revolutionized Generative AI**, allowing models to generate coherent, context-aware, and creative outputs efficiently.
