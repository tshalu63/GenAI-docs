# 06. Text Generation and NLP Applications

Generative AI excels in **text creation**, powering chatbots, content generation, summarization, and more.

---

## 1. Text Generation
- **Language Modeling:** Predict the next word or sentence.  
- **Prompt-based Generation:** Users provide instructions or context to generate relevant content.  
- **Applications:** Creative writing, automated emails, code generation.

---

## 2. NLP Applications
- **Summarization:** Condense long texts.  
- **Translation:** Cross-lingual communication.  
- **Sentiment Analysis & Personalization:** Tailor content to users.

---

## 3. Tools & Models
- **OpenAI GPT series**  
- **Hugging Face Transformers**  
- **T5, BART, LLaMA, Claude**

---

## 4. Summary
Text generation with NLP enables **human-like content creation**, knowledge summarization, and conversational AI, forming the backbone of many GenAI applications.
