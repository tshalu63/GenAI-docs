# 03. Neural Networks and Deep Learning

Neural networks are **the backbone of Generative AI**, allowing models to learn complex patterns in data.

---

## 1. What is a Neural Network?
- **Definition:** Computational models inspired by the human brain, consisting of **neurons and layers**.  
- **Types:**
  - **Feedforward Networks:** Basic structure; input → hidden layers → output.  
  - **Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs):** Excellent for images.  
  - **Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) & LSTMs:** Good for sequences like text or audio.

---

## 2. Deep Learning
- Deep learning involves **multi-layer neural networks**.  
- Enables GenAI models to:
  - Capture high-level features (images, speech, text).  
  - Generate realistic content in various modalities.

---

## 3. Role in Generative AI
- Deep learning allows **complex mapping** from inputs to outputs:
  - GANs, VAEs, and Diffusion models rely on deep architectures.  
  - Transformers (next chapter) use attention mechanisms to handle sequences efficiently.

---

## 4. Summary
Understanding **neural networks and deep learning** is key to designing, training, and fine-tuning Generative AI models.
