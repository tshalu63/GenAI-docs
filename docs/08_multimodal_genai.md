# 08. Audio and Video Generation

Generative AI is expanding into **audio and video**, creating music, speech, and realistic videos.

---

## 1. Audio Generation
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Converts text to human-like speech (e.g., Tacotron, WaveNet).  
- **Music Generation:** AI composes melodies, harmonies, and entire tracks (MusicLM, Jukebox).  
- **Voice Cloning:** Replicates a person’s voice for creative applications.

---

## 2. Video Generation
- **Video Synthesis:** AI generates videos from text prompts or images.  
- **Deepfakes:** Realistic video manipulation for entertainment and research.  
- **Future:** AI-assisted film production and virtual avatars.

---

## 3. Applications
- Podcasts, audiobooks, music production  
- Movie/animation creation  
- Virtual influencers and interactive avatars

---

## 4. Summary
Audio and video generation **extend GenAI beyond text and images**, enabling immersive, multimodal experiences.
