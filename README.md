## 📘 Overview

Welcome to **GenAI-docs** — a **comprehensive documentation repository** designed to explain **Generative AI (GenAI)** concepts in a clear, structured, and practical way.

This project covers the **core foundations**, **modern architectures**, and **real-world applications** of Generative AI models that power today’s most advanced AI systems (like ChatGPT, DALL·E, and Stable Diffusion).

---

## 🚀 What You’ll Learn

- 🌍 **Fundamentals of GenAI** — what it is, how it works, and why it matters  
- 🧩 **Model Architectures** — Transformers, Diffusion, and Large Language Models  
- 💬 **Text Generation** — prompt engineering, fine-tuning, and NLP insights  
- 🎨 **Image, Audio & Video Generation** — understanding multimodal creativity  
- ⚙️ **Frameworks & Tools** — libraries, metrics, and evaluation techniques  
- 🧭 **Ethics, Bias & Challenges** — responsible AI practices  
- 🔮 **Future Trends & Research Directions**
